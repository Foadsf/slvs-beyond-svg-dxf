What is authoritative, and what is demonstrated?

The native source defines nominal geometry and text PMI. This page and definition.json are derived views with source and export hashes. STEP is AP203 geometry only: no claim of AP242 semantic PMI, a certified Y14.41 release, or manufacturing approval. The planar 3–2–1 datum illustration assumes ideal datum features. The synthetic hole model assumes circular sections parallel to A and a straight axis; it does not model oblique cylinder sections or inspect actual form, datum establishment, roughness, or the complete part.
